# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

All accounts must use multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14
characters. Password reuse across systems is prohibited. Service accounts rotate
credentials every 90 days.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted
data (customer PII, secrets, financial records) must be encrypted at rest and in
transit and may never be copied to personal devices.

## Incident response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one
hour of discovery. The on-call responder acknowledges within 15 minutes. A
post-incident review is completed within five business days.

## Access reviews

Access to Restricted systems is reviewed quarterly. Access is revoked within 24
hours of an employee's departure.
